Privacy Policy for BLOCKS. Puzzler

Effective date: October 2, 2026

BLOCKS. Puzzler is an offline puzzle game provided by compvscom. This policy describes how the Android app handles information.

Information handled by the app

The app does not require an account. It does not collect or transmit personal information to the developer and does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

Local storage and security

The app stores your saved game, scores, achievements, unlocked themes, and preferences in its private storage on your device. This information is used to restore your game and settings. Access to this storage is protected by Android's app sandbox. The app does not send this information to a developer-operated server.

Android may include app data in device backups or transfer it when you change devices, depending on your device and backup settings. These services are governed by your device or backup provider's privacy practices.

Score sharing and clipboard

Sharing a score is optional. When you choose to share, the app passes game score and recap text to Android's share feature. If you complete sharing, the application or recipient you choose receives that text. The developer does not receive a copy. Information handled by another application is subject to that application's privacy practices.

If sharing is unavailable or fails, the app may copy the score text to your device clipboard. Clipboard access and retention are controlled by Android and any clipboard features you use. The app does not read your existing clipboard contents.

Retention and deletion

Saved game data remains in local app storage until you clear the app's storage through Android settings or uninstall the app. Starting a new game does not remove all stored scores, achievements, or preferences. Device backups may retain copies or restore data according to your backup provider's settings and policies. The developer does not hold a server-side copy of your saved game.

Privacy inquiries

You can contact compvscom at compvscom@gmail.com with privacy questions. If you email us, your email address and the information you provide will be used to respond to and resolve your inquiry. Email is handled by our email provider, Gmail. We retain correspondence only as long as needed to handle the inquiry or meet applicable legal obligations. You may use the same address to request deletion of correspondence, subject to those obligations.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. The current policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
